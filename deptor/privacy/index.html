O'zbekcha · English

Deptor — kundalik xarajatlar, berilgan va olingan qarzlar hamda avans/to'lovlarni yozib borish va hafta, oy, yil yakunida hisobot olish uchun mobil ilova. Ushbu hujjat ilova ma'lumotlaringizga qanday munosabatda bo'lishini tushuntiradi.

Qisqasi: barcha yozuvlaringiz faqat qurilmangizda saqlanadi. Ilovada ro'yxatdan o'tish, server, reklama va kuzatuvchi analitika yo'q. SMS faqat siz o'zingiz tanlagan raqamga, sizning buyrug'ingiz bilan ketadi.

01 Qanday ma'lumotlar saqlanadi

Siz ilovaga kiritgan ma'lumotlar:

  • xarajatlar: summa, toifa, izoh va sana;
  • qarzlar va avans/to'lovlar: shaxsning ismi, ixtiyoriy telefon raqami, summa, sana, qaytarish muddati va izoh;
  • sozlamalar: til, valyuta, eslatma vaqti va SMS'da ko'rinadigan ismingiz.

Bu ma'lumotlar faqat qurilmangiz ichidagi ma'lumotlar bazasida saqlanadi va hech qanday serverga yuborilmaydi. Biz ularni ko'rmaymiz.

02 SMS xabarlar

Qarz yoki avans qo'shayotganda telefon raqamini kiritib, "SMS yuborish"ni tanlasangiz, ilova shu raqamga sizning SIM kartangizdan bitta SMS yuboradi. SMS'da quyidagilar bo'ladi: siz kiritgan ismingiz, summa, sana, qaytarish muddati (bo'lsa) va yozuvni qabul qiluvchining Deptor ilovasiga qo'shish uchun havola.

  • SMS faqat siz tanlagan raqamga va faqat siz tasdiqlaganda yuboriladi.
  • SMS narxi mobil operatoringiz tarifiga ko'ra hisoblanadi.
  • Ilova kelgan SMS'laringizni o'qimaydi va SMS tarixiga kirmaydi.

03 Kontaktlar

Telefon raqamini kontaktlardan tanlash uchun ilova Android'ning standart kontakt tanlash oynasini ochadi. Ilovaga faqat siz tanlagan bitta kontaktning ismi va raqami beriladi. Ilova kontaktlar ro'yxatingizni o'qimaydi va saqlamaydi va buning uchun ruxsat so'ramaydi.

04 Havolalar

SMS'dagi havola (deptor.uz) qabul qiluvchining telefonida Deptor ilovasini ochadi va yozuvni qo'shishni taklif qiladi. Havolada faqat SMS matnidagi ma'lumotlar (ism, summa, sana) bor. Havola brauzerda ochilsa, sayt odatdagidek texnik so'rov ma'lumotlarini (IP manzil, brauzer turi) qayd etishi mumkin; biz ulardan foydalanuvchilarni aniqlash uchun foydalanmaymiz.

05 Ruxsatlar

  • SMS yuborish — siz tanlagan raqamga qarz/avans haqida xabar yuborish uchun.
  • Bildirishnomalar — kundalik eslatma, hafta/oy/yil hisobotlari va qarz muddati eslatmasi uchun.
  • Telefon yoqilganda ishga tushish — telefon qayta yoqilganda rejalashtirilgan eslatmalarni tiklash uchun.

Bu ruxsatlarning hech biri ma'lumot yig'ish yoki uzatish uchun ishlatilmaydi.

06 Uchinchi tomonlar, reklama va analitika

Ilovada reklama tarmoqlari, kuzatuvchi analitika yoki xatolarni yig'uvchi tashqi xizmatlar yo'q. Ma'lumotlaringiz sotilmaydi va hech kimga berilmaydi.

07 Ma'lumotlarni o'chirish

Har qanday yozuvni ilovaning o'zida o'chirishingiz mumkin. Ilovani telefondan o'chirsangiz, barcha ma'lumotlar ham butunlay o'chadi. Bizda ularning nusxasi yo'q.

08 Bolalar

Ilova moliyaviy yozuvlar uchun mo'ljallangan va 13 yoshgacha bo'lgan bolalarga qaratilmagan. Biz hech kimdan shaxsiy ma'lumot to'plamaymiz.

09 O'zgartirishlar

Ushbu siyosat yangilanishi mumkin. O'zgarishlar shu sahifada yangi "amal qilish sanasi" bilan e'lon qilinadi.

English

Deptor is a mobile app for recording daily expenses, money you lent or borrowed, and advances/payments, with weekly, monthly and yearly reports. This policy explains how the app handles your data.

In short: all your records stay on your device. There is no sign-up, no server, no ads and no tracking analytics. An SMS is sent only to a number you choose, only when you ask for it.

01 Data stored

  • Expenses: amount, category, note and date.
  • Debts and advances/payments: the person's name, an optional phone number, amount, date, due date and note.
  • Settings: language, currency, reminder time and the name shown in your SMS.

This data is stored only in a database on your device and is never sent to any server. We cannot see it.

02 SMS messages

If you enter a phone number for a debt or advance and choose "Send SMS", the app sends one SMS to that number from your own SIM card. It contains your name as you entered it, the amount, the date, the due date (if any) and a link that lets the recipient add the record to their own Deptor app.

  • SMS is sent only to the number you choose and only when you confirm.
  • Your mobile operator charges for the SMS according to your plan.
  • The app does not read your incoming messages or SMS history.

03 Contacts

To pick a phone number, the app opens Android's standard contact picker. Only the name and number of the single contact you select are passed to the app. The app does not read or store your contact list and does not request contacts permission.

04 Links

The link in the SMS (deptor.uz) opens Deptor on the recipient's phone and offers to add the record. It contains only the information already in the SMS text (name, amount, date). If the link is opened in a browser, the website may log standard technical request data (IP address, browser type); we do not use it to identify users.

05 Permissions

  • Send SMS — to send a debt/advance message to the number you choose.
  • Notifications — daily reminder, week/month/year reports and due-date reminders.
  • Run at startup — to restore scheduled reminders after a reboot.

None of these permissions is used to collect or transmit data.

06 Third parties, ads and analytics

The app contains no advertising networks, tracking analytics or external crash-reporting services. Your data is never sold or shared.

07 Deleting data

You can delete any record inside the app. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all of its data. We hold no copies.

08 Children

The app is meant for financial record keeping and is not directed at children under 13. We do not collect personal data from anyone.
